# B@B Voice & Copy

The words should sound like the people who ship the work: engineers and
researchers who are precise, confident, and allergic to hype. The design is
restrained and grand; the copy earns that grandeur with **specifics**.

## Principles

1. **Sentence case, everywhere.** Including headlines. "The future is on chain,"
   not "The Future Is On Chain" in your running copy (the source uses title case
   only for the single hero display line — either is fine for *the* hero, but
   default to sentence case).
2. **Proof over adjectives.** Name the real work and the real names.
   - ✅ "Members have shipped for Samsung and Arbitrum, modeled consensus attacks
     for Ripple, and built the course PayPal ran as internal training."
   - ❌ "We deliver cutting-edge, industry-leading blockchain solutions."
3. **Concrete nouns, active verbs.** Ship, model, build, audit, research, teach.
4. **Short sentences.** Let them land. Vary rhythm; end sections with momentum.
   - "We're looking for the next thing to build."
5. **Grand is allowed — once.** A declarative, slightly lofty headline works
   *because* everything around it is understated. Spend that ambition like you
   spend the gold: rarely. "The future is on chain." Then get specific.
6. **The machine voice is terse.** Mono labels are facts, not sentences:
   `EST. 2016`, `BERKELEY, CA`, `● LIVE`, `0xF4B3…0A0A`, `01 — CONSULTING`.

## Banned words

revolutionary, cutting-edge, industry-leading, next-gen, synergy, leverage (as a
verb), disrupt, unlock (as filler), seamless, robust, empower, world-class,
game-changing. If a sentence still means something with the word deleted, delete it.

## Patterns

- **Eyebrow (mono):** context in ≤4 words — `BERKELEY · EST. 2016`, `RESEARCH`,
  `CASE STUDY 03`.
- **Hero line (Garamond):** one idea, ≤8 words, declarative.
- **Lead (Instrument):** 1–2 sentences of concrete proof + a forward hook.
- **Body:** plain, specific, technical where it helps; define nothing obvious.
- **CTA labels (mono):** verbs — `Apply`, `Work with us`, `Read the report`,
  `See case studies`. Never "Learn more."
- **Metadata:** real figures — cohort size, year founded, protocols shipped,
  audits completed, students taught.

## Micro-examples

> **RESEARCH — 04**
> On-chain, but rigorous.
> We publish consensus and cryptography research with the same standard we'd
> defend in a Berkeley seminar. Peer review, not press release.
> `12 papers · 3 audits · 2016–present`

> **JOIN**
> We're looking for the next thing to build.
> Engineers, researchers, and designers. One application, one cohort a year.
> [ Apply ]  `● Applications open`

Tone summary: **a research lab that ships, writing like it has nothing to prove —
because it can point to the work.**
